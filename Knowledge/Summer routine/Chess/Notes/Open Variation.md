**In the Open Defence (_ECO_ C80-C83),** Black tries to make use of the time White will take to regain the pawn to establish a foothold in the centre. Play usually continues 6.d4 b5 7.Bb3 d5 8.dxe5 Be6. The move 8.dxe5 leads to White attacking Black's pawn on d5 with two pieces, all but forcing Black's response 8...Be6, the only way to both defend the pawn and develop a new piece. White then has a variety of options at move nine, including 9.c3, 9.Be3, 9.Qe2, and 9.Nbd2.
