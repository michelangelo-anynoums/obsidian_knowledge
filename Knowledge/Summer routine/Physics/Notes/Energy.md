# 2.3 Energy

## Energy

- **Energy** is the **stored capacity to do work**.
    
- It can be **transferred** or **changed** into other forms.
    

**Example:**

- A **battery** stores **chemical energy**.
    
- When connected to a circuit, it changes into **electrical energy**.
    

---

## Stored Capacity to Do Work

- Energy is needed to **move**, **heat**, or **change** something.
    
- If there is **no energy**, **no work** can be done.
    

**Equation:**

$$  
E = W  
$$

- **E** = Energy (**J**)
    
- **W** = Work done (**J**)
    

**Example:**

- If **20 J** of work is done to lift a box:
    

$$  
E = 20\ \text{J}  
$$

---

## Electrical Energy in Circuits

- **Electrical energy** flows through a **circuit**.
    
- It powers devices such as **lamps**, **phones**, and **computers**.
    
- It can change into **light**, **heat**, or **motion**.
    

**Equation:**

$$  
E = V \times I \times t  
$$

- **E** = Electrical energy (**J**)
    
- **V** = Voltage (**V**)
    
- **I** = Current (**A**)
    
- **t** = Time (**s**)
    

**Example:**

- A circuit has:
    
    - **V = 6 V**
        
    - **I = 2 A**
        
    - **t = 5 s**
        

$$  
E = 6 \times 2 \times 5 = 60\ \text{J}  
$$

The circuit uses **60 J** of energy.

---

## CPU Energy Consumption

- A **CPU (Central Processing Unit)** uses **electrical energy** to process data.
    
- A **busy CPU** uses **more energy** than an idle CPU.
    
- Some electrical energy becomes **heat**, so the CPU needs **cooling**.
    

**Equation:**

$$  
E = P \times t  
$$

- **E** = Energy (**J**)
    
- **P** = Power (**W**)
    
- **t** = Time (**s**)
    

**Example:**

- A CPU uses **50 W** for **10 s**.
    

$$  
E = 50 \times 10 = 500\ \text{J}  
$$

The CPU uses **500 J** of electrical energy.
