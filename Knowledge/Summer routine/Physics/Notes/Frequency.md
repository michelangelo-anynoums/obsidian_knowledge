1. # 6.2 Frequency
    
    ## **What is Frequency?**
    
    **Frequency** (**f**) is the **number of complete cycles (oscillations) in one second**.
    
    - A **cycle** is **one complete oscillation**.
    - Higher frequency → **more cycles each second**.
    - Lower frequency → **fewer cycles each second**.
    
    **Unit:** **Hertz (Hz)**
    
    - **1 Hz = 1 cycle per second**
    - **100 Hz = 100 cycles per second**
    
    ---
    
    # **Frequency Formula**
    
    The frequency is related to the **period** (**T**):
    
    f=1T
    
    where:
    
    - **f** = frequency (**Hz**)
    - **T** = period (**s**)
    
    ---
    
    # **Frequency in Technology**
    
    ## **CPU Clocks**
    
    A **CPU clock** tells how many **clock cycles** a processor performs each second.
    
    **Example:**
    
    - **3 GHz** = **3 billion cycles per second**
    
    Higher clock frequency usually means the CPU can perform **more operations each second**.
    
    ---
    
    ## **Wi-Fi Channels**
    
    Wi-Fi sends information using **radio waves**.
    
    Common frequency bands are:
    
    - **2.4 GHz**
    - **5 GHz**
    - **6 GHz**
    
    - **2.4 GHz** → longer range, lower speed.
    - **5 GHz** → shorter range, higher speed.
    - **6 GHz** → very fast, less interference.
    
    ---
    
    ## **Radio Bands**
    
    Radio stations use different **frequencies** so their signals do not mix.
    
    **Examples:**
    
    - **AM radio** → lower frequencies.
    - **FM radio** → higher frequencies.
    
    Each station broadcasts on its **own frequency**.
    
    ---
    
    # **Important Ideas**
    
    - **Frequency** = **cycles per second**.
    - **Unit:** **Hertz (Hz)**.
    - **1 Hz = 1 cycle every second**.
    - **CPU clocks**, **Wi-Fi**, and **radio** all use **frequency**.
    - Higher frequency means **more oscillations every second**.
    
    ---
    
    # **Resolved Examples**
    
    ## **Example 1**
    
    A wave makes **12 cycles in 1 second**.
    
    **Find the frequency.**
    
    **Solution**
    
    f=12 Hz
    
    **Answer:** **12 Hz**
    
    ---
    
    ## **Example 2**
    
    A wave has a **period of 0.25 s**.
    
    **Find the frequency.**
    
    **Solution**
    
    Formula:
    
    f=1T
    
    Substitute:
    
    f=10.25=4 Hz
    
    **Answer:** **4 Hz**
    
    ---
    
    ## **Example 3**
    
    A CPU has a clock speed of **2 GHz**.
    
    **How many cycles does it perform each second?**
    
    **Solution**
    
    - **1 GHz = 1 billion cycles/s**
    
    2 GHz=2×109 cycles/s
    
    **Answer:** **2 billion cycles every second.**
