# 9.2 Reflection

## **Reflection**

- **Reflection** is the **bouncing of light** from a surface.
- It happens when light hits a **smooth surface**, such as a mirror.
- Reflection allows us to **see objects**.

> **Example:** You see your face in a mirror because light is reflected.

---

## **Law of Reflection**

- The **angle of incidence** is equal to the **angle of reflection**.

### **Equation**

$$
θi=θr;  \theta_i=\theta_r
$$
Where:

- **$\theta_i$** = angle of incidence
- **$\theta_r$** = angle of reflection

![[Pasted image 20260801083312.png|499]]

---

## **Uses of Reflection**

- **Mirrors** produce clear images.
- **Fiber optics** use **total internal reflection** to carry light over long distances.
- Reflection is also used in **periscopes** and **telescopes**.

> **Example:** Internet signals travel through **fiber optic cables** using reflected light.

# **Resolved Examples**

## **Example 1: Find the angle of reflection**

A light ray strikes a mirror at an angle of **40°**.

### Given

- $\theta_i=40^\circ$

### Solution

Using the law of reflection:

$$
θi=θr\theta_i=\theta_r
$$

$$
θr=40∘\theta_r=40^\circ
$$

**Answer:** **$40^\circ$**

---

## **Example 2: Reflection from a mirror**

### Question

Why can you see yourself in a mirror?

### Solution

- Light from your face reaches the mirror.
- The mirror **reflects** the light.
- The reflected light enters your eyes.

**Answer:** You see yourself because the mirror **reflects light**.

---

## **Example 3: Fiber optics**

### Question

Why are fiber optic cables able to carry light over long distances?

### Solution

- Light **reflects repeatedly** inside the cable.
- The light remains inside the fiber.
- Very little light is lost.

**Answer:** Fiber optic cables work because light is **reflected inside the cable**.
