# 5.4 Electromagnetic Waves

## 1. **Electromagnetic Waves**

**Electromagnetic (EM) waves** are waves made of **oscillating electric fields** and **oscillating magnetic fields**.

- The **electric field** and **magnetic field** are **perpendicular** to each other.
    
- They carry **energy** through space.
    

> **Electromagnetic waves do not need a material (medium) to travel.**

---

## 2. **Combined Electric and Magnetic Oscillations**

An electromagnetic wave is formed when:

- The **electric field changes**.
    
- The **magnetic field changes**.
    
- Each changing field produces the other.
    

This allows the wave to continue moving through space.

### Formula

The speed of an electromagnetic wave is:

$$  
c=f\lambda  
$$

Where:

- **c** = speed of light (**3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**)
    
- **f** = frequency (**Hz**)
    
- **λ** = wavelength (**m**)
    

---

## 3. **Travel Through Space**

Unlike sound waves, **electromagnetic waves** can travel through a **vacuum** (empty space).

Examples:

- Sunlight travels from the **Sun** to the **Earth**.
    
- Radio waves travel through space to satellites.

---

## 4. **Basis of All Wireless Communication**

Electromagnetic waves are used to send information **without wires**.

Examples:

- **Radio**
    
- **Television**
    
- **Mobile phones**
    
- **Wi-Fi**
    
- **Bluetooth**
    
- **Satellite communication**
    

The information is carried by electromagnetic waves from a **transmitter** to a **receiver**.

---

## Important Equation

### Wave Equation

$$  
c=f\lambda  
$$

Where:

- **Higher frequency** → **Shorter wavelength**
    
- **Lower frequency** → **Longer wavelength**
    

---

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1

An electromagnetic wave has:

- Frequency = **100 Hz**
    
- Wavelength = **3.0 × 10⁶ m**
    

**Find its speed.**

### Solution

Formula:

$$  
c=f\lambda  
$$

Substitute:

$$  
c=100\times3.0\times10^6  
$$

$$  
c=3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}  
$$

**Answer:**

$$  
\boxed{c=3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}}  
$$

---

## Example 2

**Question:** Can electromagnetic waves travel through empty space?

### Solution

Yes.

Electromagnetic waves **do not need a medium**. They can travel through the **vacuum of space**.

**Answer:**

✅ **Yes, they travel through space.**

---

## Example 3

**Question:** Name two devices that use electromagnetic waves.

### Solution

Examples include:

- **Mobile phone**
    
- **Wi-Fi router**
    

Both send and receive information using electromagnetic waves.

**Answer:**

✅ **Mobile phones and Wi-Fi routers use electromagnetic waves.**

---

# Quick Summary

- **Electromagnetic waves** are made of **electric** and **magnetic** fields.
    
- The fields **oscillate** and are **perpendicular** to each other.
    
- They **travel through space** without a medium.
    
- They are used for **radio, TV, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, satellites, and mobile phones**.
    
- Main equation:
    

$$  
c=f\lambda  
$$
