# **2.2 Work**

## **Work**

- **Work** is done when a **force** moves an object.
    
- If the object **does not move**, no work is done.
    

## **Work = Force × Distance**

- **Equation:**
    
    - **Work (W) = Force (F) × Distance (d)**
        
    - **W = F × d**
        
- **Unit:** **Joule (J)**
    

**Example:**

- A **10 N** force moves a box **3 m**.
    
- **W = 10 × 3 = 30 J**
    
- The work done is **30 J**.
    

## **Energy transfer concept**

- **Work transfers energy** from one object to another.
    
- When work is done, **energy** is gained or lost.
    
- Example: Lifting a box gives it **gravitational potential energy**.

## Work

### Exercise 1

A person pushes a box with a force of **20 N** and moves it **5 m**. Calculate the work done.

**Solution:**

Formula:

**W = F × d**

Values:

- F = 20 N
    
- d = 5 m
    

**W = 20 × 5**

**W = 100 J**

**Answer:** The work done is **100 J**.

# Work = Force × Distance

### Exercise 1

A student pushes a table with a force of **30 N**, but the table does not move. Is work done?

**Solution:**

Formula:

**W = F × d**

The distance is:

**d = 0 m**

So:

**W = 30 × 0**

**W = 0 J**

**Answer:** No work is done because the object does not move.

---

### Exercise 2

Two people move the same box. Person A pushes with **40 N** for **2 m**. Person B pushes with **40 N** for **5 m**. Who does more work?

**Solution:**

Person A:

**W = 40 × 2**

**W = 80 J**

Person B:

**W = 40 × 5**

**W = 200 J**

**Answer:** Person B does more work because the box moves a longer distance.

---

### Exercise 3

A robot does **600 J** of work while moving an object **6 m**. Calculate the force used.

**Solution:**

Formula:

**F = W / d**

Values:

- W = 600 J
    
- d = 6 m
    

**F = 600 / 6**

**F = 100 N**

**Answer:** The robot applies a force of **100 N**.

# Energy Transfer Concept

### Exercise 1

A person lifts a box from the floor to a table. What happens to the energy?

**Solution:**

Work is done on the box, transferring energy to it.

**Answer:** The box gains **gravitational potential energy**.

---

### Exercise 2

A motor uses electrical energy to move a fan. What type of energy transfer happens?

**Solution:**

The motor transfers electrical energy into movement.

**Answer:** Electrical energy is changed into **kinetic energy**.

---

### Exercise 3

A student pushes a book across a table. Some energy becomes heat due to friction. Is this normal?

**Solution:**

Not all energy stays as movement energy. Some energy is transferred as heat.

**Answer:** Yes, some energy is converted into **heat energy** because of friction.
