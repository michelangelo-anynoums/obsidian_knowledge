1. # 6.4 Signal Speed
    ## **What is Signal Speed?**
    
    **Signal speed** is the **speed at which a wave travels** through a medium or space.
    
    It is also called the **wave speed**.
    
    **Unit:** **meters per second (m/s)**
    
    ---
    
    # **Wave Speed Formula**
    
    The speed of a wave depends on its **frequency** and **wavelength**.
    
    $$v=fλ$$
    
    where:
    
    - **v** = wave speed (**m/s**)
    - **f** = frequency (**Hz**)
    - **λ** = wavelength (**m**)
    
    ---
    
    # **Light Speed**
    
    Light travels at different speeds depending on the **medium**.
    
    ### **In a Vacuum**
    
    Light travels at its **maximum speed**:
    
    c=3.0×108 m/s
    
    This is about:
    
    - **300,000,000 m/s**
    - **300,000 km/s**
    
    ---
    
    ### **In Materials**
    
    Light travels **slower** in materials such as:
    
    - **Air**
    - **Water**
    - **Glass**
    
    The denser the material, the **slower** the light usually travels.
    
    **Example:**
    
    - Vacuum → fastest.
    - Water → slower.
    - Glass → even slower.
    
    ---
    
    # **Examples of Signal Speed**
    
    - **Sound in air:** about **343 m/s**
    - **Light in vacuum:** **3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**
    - **Water waves:** much slower than light.
    
    ---
    
    # **Important Ideas**
    
    - **Signal speed** is the speed of a **wave**.
    - It depends on the **medium**.
    - **Light is fastest in a vacuum**.
    - Wave speed is calculated using:
    
    $$v=fλ$$
    
    ---
    
    # **Resolved Examples**
    
    ## **Example 1**
    
    A wave has:
    
    - **f = 6 Hz**
    - **λ = 4 m**
    
    **Find the wave speed.**
    
    **Solution**
    
    Formula:
    
    v=fλ
    
    Substitute:
    
    v=6×4=24 m/s
    
    **Answer:** **24 m/s**
    
    ---
    
    ## **Example 2**
    
    A wave travels at **30 m/s** and has a wavelength of **5 m**.
    
    **Find the frequency.**
    
    **Solution**
    
    Formula:
    
    f=vλ
    
    Substitute:
    
    f=305=6 Hz
    
    **Answer:** **6 Hz**
    
    ---
    
    ## **Example 3**
    
    A light signal travels through a **vacuum** and then enters **glass**.
    
    **Which is faster?**
    
    **Solution**
    
    - In a **vacuum**, light travels at **3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**.
    - In **glass**, light travels **slower**.
    
    **Answer:** **Light is faster in a vacuum than in glass.**
