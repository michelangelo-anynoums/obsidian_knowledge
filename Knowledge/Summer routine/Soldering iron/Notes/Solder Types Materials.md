# 3. Solder Types & Materials

## Solder wire

- **Solder wire** is a metal wire used to **join electronic parts**.
    

### Leaded solder

- Contains **lead (Pb)**.
    
- **Easier to melt** and **flows smoothly**.
    
- Good for **learning and repairs**.
    

### Lead-free solder

- **Does not contain lead**.
    
- **Safer for people and the environment**.
    
- Needs a **higher temperature** and is **harder to use**.
    

## Flux

- **Flux** removes **oxidation** from metal when heating.
    
- It helps solder **stick** to the parts.

## PCB basics

- A **PCB (Printed Circuit Board)** holds and connects electronic components.
    

### Pads

- **Metal spots** where components are soldered.
    

### Traces

- **Thin copper paths** that carry electricity.
    

### Vias

- **Small holes** that connect different PCB layers.
    

## Through-hole vs SMD components

### Through-hole

- Component **leads go through holes** in the PCB.
    
- **Stronger** and easier to solder by hand.
    

### SMD (Surface-Mount Device)

- Components are soldered **directly onto the PCB surface**.
    
- **Smaller** and used in **modern electronics**.
