# 2. Tools & Equipment

## Essential tools

### **Soldering iron**

- Used to **heat the metal** so the solder can melt.
    
- A **temperature-controlled** iron is best.
    

### **Solder wire**

- A **metal wire** that melts to join two metal parts.
    
- Can be **lead** or **lead-free**.
    

### **Flux**

- **Flux** cleans the metal.
    
- It helps the solder **flow** and **stick** better.
    

### **Soldering stand + sponge or brass wool**

- The **stand** holds the hot soldering iron safely.
    
- The **sponge** or **brass wool** cleans the iron tip.
    

### **Wire cutters (flush cutters)**

- Used to **cut wires** and remove extra wire.
    

### **Tweezers**

- Used to **hold small parts** safely.
    

## Important notes

### **Temperature**

- **Lead solder:** about **320–350°C**.
    
- **Lead-free solder:** about **350–400°C**.
    

### **Iron tip types**

#### **Conical tip**

- **Pointed tip**.
    
- Good for **small** and **precise** work.
    

#### **Chisel tip**

- **Flat tip**.
    
- Good for **most electronic work**.
    
- **Best choice for beginners**.
