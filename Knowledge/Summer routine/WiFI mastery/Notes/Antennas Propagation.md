# Antennas + Propagation

## **Omnidirectional Antennas**

- **Omnidirectional antennas** send and receive signals in all directions.
- They provide wide coverage but usually have less range.
- They are common in Wi-Fi routers and mobile devices.

## **Directional Antennas**

- **Directional antennas** focus signals in one specific direction.
- They provide longer range and stronger signals in that direction.
- They are used for point-to-point communication.

## **Reflection**

- **Reflection** happens when a signal hits a surface and bounces back.
- Buildings, walls, and metal objects can cause reflection.
- It can create signal problems or multiple signal paths.

## **Absorption**

- **Absorption** happens when materials take in signal energy.
- Walls, water, and human bodies can weaken signals.
- More absorption means a weaker connection.

![[Pasted image 20260723132042.png|545]]

## **Diffraction**

- **Diffraction** happens when signals bend around obstacles.
- It allows signals to reach areas without a direct path.
- The signal becomes weaker after diffraction.
