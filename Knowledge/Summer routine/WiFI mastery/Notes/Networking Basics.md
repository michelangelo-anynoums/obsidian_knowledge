# Networking Basics

## What is a Network?

A **network** is a group of **computers** and **devices** connected together. They can **share data**, **files**, and **resources** like printers or the internet.

## LAN vs WAN

### **LAN (Local Area Network)**

- Covers a **small area** (home, school, office).
    
- Has **fast** connections.
    
- Usually owned by **one person or organization**.
    

### **WAN (Wide Area Network)**

- Covers a **large area** (cities, countries, or the world).
    
- Connects many **LANs** together.
    
- Usually slower than a LAN.
    

## Internet

The **Internet** is the **largest network** in the world. It connects **millions of devices** and lets people **share information** and **communicate**.

## Clients

A **client** is a **device or program** that **requests services or data** from a server.

**Example:** A web browser asking for a website.

## Servers

A **server** is a **computer or program** that **provides services or data** to clients.

**Example:** A web server sends web pages to your browser.

## Network Topologies

A **network topology** is the **way devices are connected** in a network.

Common types:

- **Star** – All devices connect to one central device.
    
- **Bus** – All devices share one main cable.
    
- **Ring** – Devices connect in a circle.
    
- **Mesh** – Devices have many direct connections to each other.
