
# Mechanical Mouse

- Uses a **physical ball** to detect movement.
    
- The ball rotates as the mouse moves.
    
- **Largely obsolete** today.
    

**Example:** Early Microsoft or Logitech ball mouse.

**Mouse example to buy:**

- Mainly available as **used or vintage** models.
    
- Not recommended for modern use.

# Optical Mouse

![[Pasted image 20260710111111.png|375]]

- Uses an **LED sensor**.
    
- **Most common** type of mouse.
    
- Works well on most surfaces.
    

**Example:** A standard office mouse.

**Mouse example to buy:**

- **Logitech M100**
    
- **Logitech B100**
    
- **HP X500**
    

---

# Laser Mouse

![[Pasted image 20260710111337.png|272]]

- Uses a **laser sensor**.
    
- Works on **more surfaces** than optical mice.
    
- More precise for some tasks.
    

**Example:** A professional office mouse.

**Mouse example to buy:**

- **Logitech MX Master 3S**
    
- **Logitech MX Anywhere 3S**

# Wired Mouse

![[Pasted image 20260710111430.png|257]]

- Connects with a **USB cable**.
    
- **No battery** required.
    
- Reliable with **low input delay**.
    

**Example:** A desktop computer mouse.

**Mouse example to buy:**

- **Logitech G203**
    
- **Razer DeathAdder Essential**
    
- **Logitech M100**
    

---

# Wireless Mouse

![[Pasted image 20260710111456.png|336]]

- Connects using **Bluetooth** or a **USB receiver**.
    
- **Portable** and cable-free.
    
- Needs **batteries** or **recharging**.
    

**Example:** A laptop mouse.

**Mouse example to buy:**

- **Logitech M185**
    
- **Logitech Pebble Mouse 2 M350s**
    
- **Microsoft Bluetooth Mouse**

# Trackball Mouse

![[Pasted image 20260710111658.png|344]]

- The mouse **stays in one place**.
    
- Move the cursor by rolling a **ball with your fingers**.
    
- Good for **small desks** and ergonomic setups.
    

**Example:** A workstation trackball.

**Mouse example to buy:**

- **Logitech ERGO M575**
    
- **Kensington Expert Mouse Wireless Trackball**
