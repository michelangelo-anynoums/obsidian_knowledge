# Motherboard

The **motherboard** is the **main circuit board** of a computer. It **connects** all computer parts so they can work together.

## Purpose

- The **purpose** of the motherboard is to **connect** and **control communication** between all hardware.
    

## Chipset

- The **chipset** manages **communication** between the **CPU**, **RAM**, **storage**, and other devices.
    
- It also controls **which features** the motherboard supports.
    

## CPU Socket

- The **CPU socket** is the **place where the CPU is installed**.
    
- The **socket type** must **match the CPU**.
    

## RAM Slots

- **RAM slots** hold the **memory (RAM)** modules.
    
- More **RAM slots** allow **more memory** to be installed.
    

## Storage Connectors

- **Storage connectors** connect **HDDs**, **SSDs**, and other storage devices.
    
- Common connectors are **SATA** and **M.2**.
    

## Expansion Slots

- **Expansion slots** let you add **extra hardware**.
    
- They are used for **graphics cards**, **sound cards**, **network cards**, and more.
    
- The most common type is **PCIe (PCI Express)**.
