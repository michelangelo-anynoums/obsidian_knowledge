# 11.1 Radio Waves

## 📡 What are radio waves?

**Radio waves** are a type of **electromagnetic wave**.

They can travel through **empty space**, so they do not need a material medium.

They have:

- **Electric field** and **magnetic field**.
- A **long wavelength** compared with many other electromagnetic waves.
- A **low frequency** compared with higher-frequency electromagnetic waves.

The wave speed in vacuum is:

$$c=3.0×10^8m/sc = 3.0 \times 10^8\ {m/s}$$

The relationship between speed, frequency and wavelength is:

$$c=fλ$$

where:

- $c$ = speed of light
- $f$ = frequency in Hz
- $\lambda$ = wavelength in m

## 📶 Uses of radio waves

Radio waves are used to **send information without wires**.

Examples:

- **Wi-Fi** → sends data between devices and a router.
- **Bluetooth** → connects nearby devices.
- **GPS** → uses radio signals from satellites to help find a position.

> **Important:** Radio waves can carry information by changing properties of the wave, such as its **amplitude, frequency, or phase**.

## 🧮 Resolved example 1 — Finding wavelength

A radio wave has a frequency of $100\ \text{MHz}$. Find its wavelength.

First convert the frequency:

$$100MHz=100×106Hz100\ {MHz} =100\times 10^6{Hz}$$

Use:

$$λ=cf\lambda = \frac{c}{f}$$

$$λ=3.0×108100×106\lambda =\frac{3.0\times10^8}{100\times10^6}$$

$$λ=3.0m\boxed {\lambda=3.0m}$$

**Answer:** The wavelength is **3.0 m**.

## 🧮 Resolved example 2 — Finding frequency

A radio wave has a wavelength of $2\ \text{m}$. Find its frequency.

Use:

$$f=cλf = \frac{c}{\lambda}$$

$$f=3.0×1082f = \frac{3.0\times10^8}{2}$$

$$
f=1.5×108Hz\boxed{f=1.5\times10^8Hz}
$$

or:

$$f=150MHz\boxed{f=150MHz}$$

**Answer:** The frequency is **150 MHz**.

## 🧮 Resolved example 3 — Simple application

A Wi-Fi signal has a frequency of $2.4\ \text{GHz}$. Find its wavelength.

Convert:

$$
2.4GHz=2.4×109Hz2.4GHz=2.4\times10^9Hz
$$

Then:

$$λ=3.0×1082.4×109\lambda=\frac{3.0\times10^8}{2.4\times10^9}$$

$$λ≈0.125m\boxed{\lambda\approx 0.125m}$$

So:

$$0.125m=12.5cm0.125m = 12.5cm$$

**Answer:** The wavelength is approximately **12.5 cm**.
