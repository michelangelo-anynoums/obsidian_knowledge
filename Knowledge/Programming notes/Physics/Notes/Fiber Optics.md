# 9.4 Fiber Optics

## **Fiber Optics**

- **Fiber optics** is a technology that uses **light signals** to transmit information.
- Data travels through very thin strands of **glass or plastic fibers**.
- Light stays inside the fiber using **total internal reflection**.

> **Example:** Internet cables use fiber optics to send data quickly.

---

## **Light-Based Data Transmission**

- Information is converted into **light pulses**.
- These light pulses travel through the fiber cable.
- A receiver changes the light signals back into **electronic data**.

> **Example:** A video call uses fiber optic cables to send voice and images as light signals.

---

## **Very High Bandwidth Communication**

- **Bandwidth** is the amount of data that can be transmitted in a given time.
- Fiber optics has **very high bandwidth** because light can carry a large amount of information.
- It allows **fast internet**, long-distance communication, and reliable data transfer.

---

## **Important Facts**

- ✅ **Fiber optics uses light to transmit data.**
- ✅ **Total internal reflection keeps light inside the fiber.**
- ✅ **Fiber optics provides very high-speed communication.**
- ✅ **It is used for internet and telecommunications.**

## **Example 2: Why does fiber optics use light?**

### Question

Why are light signals used in fiber optic cables?

### Solution

- Light travels very fast.
- Light can carry a lot of information.
- It has low energy loss over long distances.

**Answer:** Light allows **fast and efficient data transmission**.

---

## **Example 3: Total internal reflection**

### Question

Why does light stay inside a fiber optic cable?

### Solution

- The fiber has a glass core surrounded by another material.
- Light reflects repeatedly inside the core.
- The signal travels through the cable without escaping.

**Answer:** Light stays inside because of **total internal reflection**.
