# 3.3 Current

## **Current**

- **Electric current** is the **flow of electric charge** through a conductor.
    
- Current flows when there is a **closed circuit**.
    
- The **unit of current** is the **ampere (A)**.
    

**Formula:**

```text
I = Q / t
```

Where:

- **I** = current (**A**)
    
- **Q** = electric charge (**C**)
    
- **t** = time (**s**)
    

**Example:**

- If **10 C** of charge pass through a wire in **2 s**, the current is **5 A**.
    

---

**André-Marie Ampère** was a French scientist who discovered how **electric current** creates magnetic forces. He figured out the rules for how electricity flows through wires and interacts with magnets. Because of his work, the unit used to measure the **strength of electric current** is named the **ampere** (or amp).

---

## **Flow of Electric Charge**

- Current is the **movement of electric charges**.
    
- In **metal wires**, the moving charges are **electrons**.
    
- A **larger current** means **more charge flows each second**.
    

**Example:**

- A thick wire can carry **more current** than a thin wire.
    

---

## **Measured in Amperes**

- Current is measured in **amperes (A)**, often called **amps**.
    
- An **ammeter** measures the current in a circuit.
    
- **1 ampere** means **1 coulomb of charge flows every second**.
    

**Formula:**

```text
1 A = 1 C/s
```

**Example:**

- A current of **2 A** means **2 C** of charge pass through the wire every second.
    

---

## **Electron Flow in Conductors**

- In **conductors** (such as **copper**), **electrons move freely**.
    
- The movement of electrons creates an **electric current**.
    
- **Electrons flow from the negative (−) terminal to the positive (+) terminal.**
    
- **Conventional current** is defined in the **opposite direction**, from **positive (+)** to **negative (−)**.
    

> **Remember:**
> 
> - **Electron flow:** **− → +**
>     
> - **Conventional current:** **+ → −**
>     

**Example:**

- In a battery circuit, electrons leave the **negative terminal**, travel through the wire, and return to the **positive terminal**.

# 3.3 Current — Resolved Exercises

## Current

### Exercise 1

A charge of **20 C** flows through a wire in **4 s**. Calculate the current.

**Solution:**

Formula:

**I = Q / t**

**I = 20 / 4**

**I = 5 A**

**Answer:** The current is **5 A**.

---

### Exercise 2

A circuit has a current of **3 A** for **10 s**. How much charge flows?

**Solution:**

Formula:

**Q = I × t**

**Q = 3 × 10**

**Q = 30 C**

**Answer:** **30 C** of charge flows.
