# 5.1 Electric Fields

![[Pasted image 20260722073050.png|697]]
## 1. **Electric Field**

An **electric field** is the **space around a charged object** where other charges feel an electric force.

- A **positive charge** creates a field that points **away** from it.
    
- A **negative charge** creates a field that points **towards** it.
    

> **An electric field allows charges to interact even when they are not touching.**

## 2. **Fields Created by Charges**

Every **electric charge** produces an **electric field** around itself.

The strength of the field becomes **weaker** as the distance from the charge increases.

### Formula

$$  
E=\frac{F}{q}  
$$

Where:

- **E** = electric field strength (**N/C**)
    
- **F** = electric force (**N**)
    
- **q** = test charge (**C**)
    

---

## 3. **Force Without Contact**

Electric charges can **push** or **pull** each other **without touching**.

This happens because of the **electric field**.

- **Like charges** (+,+ or −,−) **repel** each other.
    
- **Unlike charges** (+,−) **attract** each other.
    

### Example

A balloon rubbed on your hair sticks to a wall because of **electric forces** acting without contact.

## 4. **Field Direction and Strength**

### **Direction**

The electric field direction is the direction a **positive test charge** would move.

- From **positive → away**
    
- Towards **negative → toward**
    

### **Strength**

The electric field is:

- **Stronger** near the charge.
    
- **Weaker** farther away.
    

Field lines help show this.

- **Lines close together** → stronger field.
    
- **Lines far apart** → weaker field.
    

---

## Important Equations

### Electric field

$$  
E=\frac{F}{q}  
$$

### Rearranged formulas

$$  
F=Eq  
$$

$$  
q=\frac{F}{E}  
$$

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1

A force of **6 N** acts on a **2 C** charge.

**Find the electric field strength.**

### Solution

Formula:

$$  
E=\frac{F}{q}  
$$

Substitute:

$$  
E=\frac{6}{2}=3\ \text{N/C}  
$$

**Answer:**

$$  
\boxed{E=3\ \text{N/C}}  
$$

---

## Example 2

An electric field has a strength of **8 N/C**.

A charge of **4 C** is placed inside it.

**Find the electric force.**

### Solution

Formula:

$$  
F=Eq  
$$

Substitute:

$$  
F=8\times4=32\ \text{N}  
$$

**Answer:**

$$  
\boxed{F=32\ \text{N}}  
$$

---

## Example 3

A force of **15 N** acts in an electric field of **5 N/C**.

**Find the charge.**

### Solution

Formula:

$$  
q=\frac{F}{E}  
$$

Substitute:

$$  
q=\frac{15}{5}=3\ \text{C}  
$$

**Answer:**

$$  
\boxed{q=3\ \text{C}}  
$$

---

# Quick Summary

- **Electric field** = region where charges feel a force.
    
- **Every charge creates an electric field.**
    
- Charges can **attract or repel without touching.**
    
- Field direction is the direction a **positive charge** moves.
    
- **Stronger field** = closer to the charge.
    
- Main formula:
    

$$  
E=\frac{F}{q}  
$$
