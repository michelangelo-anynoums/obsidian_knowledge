# 8.1 Conductors

## Conductors

- **Conductors** are **materials that allow electric current to flow easily**.
    
- They have many **free electrons** that can move through the material.
    
- Because of this, conductors have **low electrical resistance**.
    

**Examples:** Copper, aluminum, silver.

## Free Electrons

- **Free electrons** are electrons that **can move freely** inside a conductor.
    
- When a **voltage** is applied, these electrons move and create an **electric current**.
    

### Simple idea

- **No voltage** → electrons move randomly.
    
- **With voltage** → electrons move in one direction.
    

### Equation

$$I=\frac{Q}{t}$$

Where:

- **I** = current (A)
    
- **Q** = electric charge (C)
    
- **t** = time (s)

## Low Resistance Materials

Some materials let current flow very easily because they have **low resistance**.

### **Copper (Cu)**

- **Very good conductor.**
    
- Used in **electrical wires**.
    
- Strong and long-lasting.
    

### **Aluminum (Al)**

- Also a **good conductor**.
    
- **Lighter** and **cheaper** than copper.
    
- Used in **power transmission lines**.
    

### Comparison

|Material|Resistance|Common use|
|---|---|---|
|**Copper**|Very low|House wiring|
|**Aluminum**|Low|Power lines|

## Important Equation

### Ohm's Law

$$V=IR$$

Where:

- **V** = voltage (V)
    
- **I** = current (A)
    
- **R** = resistance (Ω)
    

**Idea:** If **resistance is low**, **current flows more easily**.

---

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1: Find the current

A wire carries **12 C** of charge in **4 s**.

**Given:**

- (Q=12,C)
    
- (t=4,s)
    

**Formula:**

$$I=\frac{Q}{t}$$

**Solution:**

$$I=\frac{12}{4}=3\ A$$

**Answer:** **Current = 3 A**

---

## Example 2: Find the resistance

A wire has **12 V** across it and a current of **3 A**.

**Given:**

- (V=12,V)
    
- (I=3,A)
    

**Formula:**

$$R=\frac{V}{I}$$

**Solution:**

$$R=\frac{12}{3}=4\ \Omega$$

**Answer:** **Resistance = 4 Ω**

---

## Example 3: Which material is better?

You need to make a wire for a house.

Options:

- Copper
    
- Aluminum
    

**Solution:**

- **Copper** has **very low resistance** and is commonly used for house wiring.
    
- **Aluminum** is lighter and is mainly used for long power lines.
    

**Answer:** **Copper** is the better choice for house wiring.

---

# Key Points

- **Conductors** allow **electric current** to flow easily.
    
- **Free electrons** move through conductors.
    
- **Copper** and **aluminum** are **low resistance materials**.
    
- **Low resistance** means **current flows more easily**.
    
- **Ohm's Law:** **(V=IR)**
    
- **Current equation:** **(I=\frac{Q}{t})**
