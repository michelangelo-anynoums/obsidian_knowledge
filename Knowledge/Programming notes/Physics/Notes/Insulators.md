# 8.2 Insulators

## Insulators

- **Insulators** are **materials that do not allow electric current to flow easily**.
    
- They have **very few or no free electrons**.
    
- Because of this, they have **high electrical resistance**.
    

**Examples:** Plastic, rubber, glass, wood, air.

---

## No Free Charge Movement

- In an **insulator**, the electrons are **tightly held** by the atoms.
    
- They **cannot move freely** through the material.
    
- Therefore, **electric charge does not flow easily**.
    

### Simple idea

- **Conductors** → free electrons move easily.
    
- **Insulators** → electrons stay in place.
    

---

## Prevent Current Flow

- **Insulators prevent electric current from flowing.**
    
- They are used to **protect people** from electric shocks.
    
- They also **cover electrical wires** to keep the current inside the wire.
    

### Examples

- **Plastic** around electrical cables.
    
- **Rubber** gloves for electricians.
    
- **Glass** used in some electrical equipment.
    

---

## Important Equation

### Ohm's Law

$$V=IR$$

Where:

- **V** = voltage (V)
    
- **I** = current (A)
    
- **R** = resistance (Ω)
    

**Idea:** An **insulator has a very high resistance**, so **very little current flows**.

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1: Identify the insulator

Which material is an **insulator**?

- Copper
    
- Plastic
    
- Aluminum
    

**Solution:**

- Copper and aluminum are **conductors**.
    
- Plastic **does not allow current to flow easily**.
    

**Answer:** **Plastic**

---

## Example 2: Which material protects electrical wires?

A wire is covered with **rubber**.

Why?

**Solution:**

- Rubber is an **insulator**.
    
- It **prevents current from leaving the wire**.
    
- It helps **protect people from electric shock**.
    

**Answer:** **Rubber is used because it is an insulator.**

---

## Example 3: Compare two materials

Which material allows current to flow more easily?

- Glass
    
- Copper
    

**Solution:**

- Glass is an **insulator**.
    
- Copper is a **conductor** with many **free electrons**.
    

**Answer:** **Copper allows current to flow more easily.**

---

# Key Points

- **Insulators** have **no free charge movement**.
    
- They have **high electrical resistance**.
    
- They **prevent electric current from flowing**.
    
- Common insulators are **plastic, rubber, glass, wood, and air**.
    
- Insulators are used for **safety** and to **cover electrical wires**.
