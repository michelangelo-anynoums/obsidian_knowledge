# 2.4 Power

## Power

- **Power** is the **rate of using or transferring energy**.
    
- It tells us **how fast energy is used**.
    

**Equation:**

$$  
P = \frac{E}{t}
$$

#### The formula can also be: **P = V (voltage) * I (current)**

- **P** = Power (**W**)
    
- **E** = Energy (**J**)
    
- **t** = Time (**s**)
    

**Example:**

- A lamp uses **100 J** of energy in **5 s**.
    

$$  
P = \frac{100}{5} = 20\ \text{W}  
$$

The lamp has a **power of 20 W**.

---

## Power = Energy / Time

- A device that uses **more energy in less time** has **higher power**.
    
- A device that uses **the same energy over a longer time** has **lower power**.
    

**Example:**

- Device A: **100 J** in **5 s**
    

$$  
P = \frac{100}{5} = 20\ \text{W}  
$$

- Device B: **100 J** in **10 s**
    

$$  
P = \frac{100}{10} = 10\ \text{W}  
$$

**Device A** is more powerful because it uses energy **faster**.

---

## Watt as Unit of Computing Consumption

- The **watt (W)** is the **unit of power**.
    
- It shows **how much electrical power** a computer or device uses.
    
- **1 watt = 1 joule per second**.
    

**Equation:**

$$  
1\ \text{W} = 1\ \text{J/s}  
$$

**Example:**

- A **60 W** monitor uses **60 J** of energy every **second**.
    

---

## Heat Generation in Electronics

- Electronic devices **do not convert all energy into useful work**.
    
- Some electrical energy becomes **heat**.
    
- **CPUs** and **GPUs** need **cooling systems** to remove this heat.
    

**Example:**

- A CPU uses **80 W** of power.
    
- Most of this power is eventually released as **heat**, so a **fan** or **heat sink** keeps the CPU cool.

# 2.4 Power — Resolved Exercises

## Power

### Exercise 1

A computer uses **200 J** of energy in **10 s**. Calculate its power.

**Solution:**

Formula:

**P = E / t**

Values:

- E = 200 J
    
- t = 10 s
    

**P = 200 / 10 = 20 W**

**Answer:** The computer has a power of **20 W**.

---

### Exercise 2

A lamp has a power of **50 W** and works for **4 seconds**. How much energy does it use?

**Solution:**

Formula:

**E = P × t**

Values:

- P = 50 W
    
- t = 4 s
    

**E = 50 × 4 = 200 J**

**Answer:** The lamp uses **200 J** of energy.

---

### Exercise 3

A device uses **600 J** of energy in **30 seconds**. Calculate its power.

**Solution:**

Formula:

**P = E / t**

**P = 600 / 30 = 20 W**

**Answer:** The device has a power of **20 W**.

# Heat Generation in Electronics

### Exercise 1

A CPU uses **100 W** of power. Most of this energy becomes heat. Why does it need cooling?

**Solution:**

Electronic devices are not 100% efficient. Some electrical energy changes into heat.

**Answer:** The CPU needs cooling to remove heat and prevent damage.

---

### Exercise 2

A graphics card produces a lot of heat while gaming. What component can help remove this heat?

**Solution:**

Cooling systems remove heat from electronic components.

**Answer:** A **fan** or **heat sink** can remove the heat.

---

### Exercise 3

A device uses **90 W** of power. After some time, most of this energy becomes heat. Is this normal?

**Solution:**

Electrical devices always lose some energy as heat.

**Answer:** Yes, this is normal because some electrical energy is converted into heat.
