# 9.3 Refraction

## **Refraction**

- **Refraction** is the **bending of light** when it passes from one material to another.
- It happens because the **speed of light changes** in different materials.
- Light bends **towards the normal** when it enters a **denser** material.
- Light bends **away from the normal** when it enters a **less dense** material.

> **Example:** A straw in a glass of water looks bent because of **refraction**.

## **Refractive Index**

The **refractive index** shows how much light slows down in a material.

### **Equation**

$$
n=cvn=\frac{c}{v}
$$
Where:

- **$n$** = refractive index
- **$c$** = speed of light in vacuum = **$3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$**
- **$v$** = speed of light in the material

---

## **Uses of Refraction**

- **Lenses** in glasses and cameras.
- **Microscopes** and **telescopes**.
- **Optical signal routing** in communication systems using optical devices.

> **Example:** Eyeglasses use **refraction** to help people see clearly.

---

## **Important Facts**

- ✅ **Refraction is the bending of light.**
- ✅ **Light changes speed in different materials.**
- ✅ **Refraction is used in lenses and optical communication.**

# **Resolved Examples**

## **Example 1: Find the refractive index**

Light travels in glass at **$2.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$**.

### Given

- $c=3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$
- $v=2.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$

### Solution

$$
n=cvn=\frac{c}{v}
$$

$$
n=3.0×1082.0×108n=\frac{3.0\times10^8}{2.0\times10^8}
$$

$$
n=1.5n=1.5
$$

**Answer:** **$n=1.5$**

## **Example 2: Light entering water**

### Question

What happens when light travels from air into water?

### Solution

- Water is **optically denser** than air.
- Light **slows down**.
- The light ray **bends towards the normal**.

**Answer:** The light **bends towards the normal**.

---

## **Example 3: Everyday refraction**

### Question

Why does a pencil in a glass of water look bent?

### Solution

- Light travels from **water to air**.
- The light **changes speed**.
- The light **bends** before reaching your eyes.

**Answer:** The pencil appears bent because of **refraction of light**.
