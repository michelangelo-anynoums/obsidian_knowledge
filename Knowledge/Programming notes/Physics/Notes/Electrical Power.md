# 3.6 Electrical Power

## What is **Electrical Power**?

**Electrical power** is the **rate at which electrical energy is used or transferred**.

It tells us **how fast** a device uses electricity.

The unit of power is the **Watt (W)**.

---

# **Power = Voltage × Current**

The basic formula is:

`P = V × I`

Where:

- **P** = **Power** (Watts, **W**)
    
- **V** = **Voltage** (Volts, **V**)
    
- **I** = **Current** (Amperes, **A**)
    

Other useful formulas:

`V = P / I`

`I = P / V`

---

# Why Devices **Heat Up**

When **electric current** flows through a device, it meets **resistance**.

The resistance changes some **electrical energy** into **heat energy**.

This is called the **heating effect of electricity**.

### Simple examples

- A **heater** uses heat on purpose.
    
- A **light bulb** becomes warm.
    
- A **phone charger** may get slightly hot after long use.
    

---

# **Energy Efficiency** in Systems

**Efficiency** tells us how much **useful energy** we get compared to the **total energy** used.

A device with **high efficiency** wastes **less energy**, usually as heat.

### Efficiency formula

Efficiency = (Useful Energy Output / Total Energy Input) × 100%

### Example

A motor uses **100 J** of energy and produces **80 J** of useful work.

Efficiency = (80 / 100) × 100%

Efficiency = 80%

This means **20%** of the energy is wasted, mostly as **heat**.

---

# Important Ideas

- **Power = Voltage × Current**
    
- Power is measured in **Watts (W)**.
    
- Resistance causes some electrical energy to become **heat**.
    
- Efficient devices waste **less energy**.
    
- Most wasted energy becomes **heat**.

# Resolved Example 1

**Find the power.**

Given:

- `V = 12 V`
    
- `I = 2 A`
    

Formula:

`P = V × I`

Calculation:

`P = 12 × 2`

`P = 24 W`

**Answer:** **P = 24 W**

# Resolved Example 2

**Find the current.**

Given:

- `P = 60 W`
    
- `V = 12 V`
    

Formula:

`I = P / V`

Calculation:

`I = 60 / 12`

`I = 5 A`

**Answer:** **I = 5 A**

# Resolved Example 3

**Find the efficiency.**

Given:

- **Useful Energy Output = 90 J**
    
- **Total Energy Input = 120 J**
    

Formula:

`Efficiency = (Useful Energy Output / Total Energy Input) × 100%`

Calculation:

`Efficiency = (90 / 120) × 100%`

`Efficiency = 0.75 × 100%`

`Efficiency = 75%`

**Answer:** **Efficiency = 75%**
