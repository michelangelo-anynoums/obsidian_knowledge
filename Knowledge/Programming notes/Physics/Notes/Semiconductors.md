# 8.3 Semiconductors

## Semiconductors

- **Semiconductors** are materials with **controlled conductivity**.
    
- They conduct electricity **better than insulators**, but **not as well as conductors**.
    
- Their conductivity can **change** under different conditions.
    

**Examples:** Silicon (Si), germanium (Ge).

---

## Controlled Conductivity

- **Controlled conductivity** means the material can **allow more or less current to flow**.
    
- Conductivity can change by:
    
    - **Adding impurities (doping)**.
        
    - **Increasing temperature**.
        
    - **Using light**.
        

### Simple idea

- **Conductor** → current flows easily.
    
- **Semiconductor** → current flow can be controlled.
    
- **Insulator** → current hardly flows.
    

---

## Silicon-Based Systems

- **Silicon (Si)** is the **most common semiconductor**.
    
- It is used to make:
    
    - **Computer chips**
        
    - **Transistors**
        
    - **Diodes**
        
    - **Solar cells**
        

**Why silicon?**

- It is **abundant**, **cheap**, and **easy to control**.
    

---

## Important Equation

### Ohm's Law

$$V=IR$$

Where:

- **V** = voltage (V)
    
- **I** = current (A)
    
- **R** = resistance (Ω)
    

**Idea:** A **semiconductor** has a **medium resistance**, and its resistance **can change** depending on conditions.

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1: Identify the semiconductor

Which material is a **semiconductor**?

- Copper
    
- Silicon
    
- Plastic
    

**Solution:**

- Copper is a **conductor**.
    
- Plastic is an **insulator**.
    
- Silicon is a **semiconductor**.
    

**Answer:** **Silicon**

---

## Example 2: Compare materials

Arrange these materials from **best conductor** to **best insulator**.

- Copper
    
- Silicon
    
- Rubber
    

**Solution:**

- Copper conducts electricity very well.
    
- Silicon has controlled conductivity.
    
- Rubber prevents current flow.
    

**Answer:**

**Copper → Silicon → Rubber**

---

## Example 3: Uses of silicon

A computer processor is made from **silicon**.

Why?

**Solution:**

- Silicon is a **semiconductor**.
    
- Its conductivity can be **controlled**.
    
- This makes it ideal for **electronic circuits and computer chips**.
    

**Answer:** **Silicon is used because its conductivity can be controlled.**

---

# Key Points

- **Semiconductors** have **controlled conductivity**.
    
- They conduct **better than insulators** but **worse than conductors**.
    
- **Silicon** is the most widely used semiconductor.
    
- Semiconductors are used in **computer chips, transistors, diodes, and solar cells**.
    
- Their conductivity can change with **doping, temperature, or light**.
