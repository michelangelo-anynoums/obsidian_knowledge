# 11.4 Interference

## 📡 What is interference?

**Interference** happens when **unwanted signals overlap** with the signal we want to receive.

It can make communication **less clear** or cause **data errors**.

### 🔊 Noise in wireless systems

**Noise** is unwanted electrical or electromagnetic energy that can affect a wireless signal.

Examples:

- Another Wi-Fi network using the same frequency.
- Other electronic devices producing unwanted signals.
- Natural electromagnetic signals.

> **Important:** More interference usually means **lower signal quality** and a greater chance of errors.

## 🧮 Resolved example 1

Two Wi-Fi networks use the **same frequency** in the same area.

**Question:** What can happen?

**Solution:**

Their signals can **overlap**, causing interference.

The connection may become **slower or less reliable**.
