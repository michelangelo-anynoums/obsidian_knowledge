# 5.2 Magnetic Fields

## 1. **Magnetic Fields**

A **magnetic field** is the **space around a magnet or a moving electric charge** where magnetic forces can act.

- Magnetic fields are produced by **magnets** and **moving charges (electric current)**.
    
- The symbol for magnetic field is **B**.
    

> **A magnetic field acts on moving charges and magnets.**

## 2. **Produced by Moving Charges (Current)**

A **moving electric charge** creates a **magnetic field**.

- A **wire carrying current** produces a magnetic field around it.
    
- A **larger current** produces a **stronger magnetic field**.
    

### Formula

$$  
B=\frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}  
$$

Where:

- **B** = magnetic field strength (**T**, tesla)
    
- **μ₀** = permeability of free space
    
- **I** = electric current (**A**)
    
- **r** = distance from the wire (**m**)
    

---

## 3. **Interaction with Electric Fields**

Electric and magnetic fields are closely related.

- A **moving charge** creates a **magnetic field**.
    
- A **moving charge inside a magnetic field** experiences a **magnetic force**.
    

Together, electric and magnetic fields form an **electromagnetic field**.

### Example

A wire carrying current near a magnet experiences a **force**.

## 4. **Basis of Motors and Storage**

Magnetic fields are used in many everyday devices.

Examples:

- **Electric motors** → magnetic fields make the motor spin.
    
- **Generators** → convert motion into electricity.
    
- **Hard drives** → store information using magnetic materials.
    
- **Speakers** → use magnets to produce sound.
    

---

## Important Equation

### Magnetic field around a straight wire

$$  
B=\frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}  
$$

**Key ideas:**

- Larger **current (I)** → stronger magnetic field.
    
- Larger **distance (r)** → weaker magnetic field.

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1

A wire carries an electric current.

**Question:** Does it produce a magnetic field?

### Solution

Yes.

A **moving electric charge (current)** always produces a **magnetic field** around the wire.

**Answer:**

✅ **Yes, a magnetic field is produced.**

---

## Example 2

A current in a wire increases from **2 A** to **4 A**.

**Question:** What happens to the magnetic field?

### Solution

A larger current creates a **stronger magnetic field**.

Since the current doubles, the magnetic field also becomes **stronger**.

**Answer:**

✅ **The magnetic field increases.**

---

## Example 3

An electric motor is connected to a battery.

**Question:** Why does the motor spin?

### Solution

The **electric current** creates a **magnetic field**.

This magnetic field interacts with the magnets inside the motor, producing a **force** that makes the motor rotate.

**Answer:**

✅ **The motor spins because magnetic fields create a turning force.**

---

# Quick Summary

- A **magnetic field** is produced by **magnets** and **moving charges**.
    
- An **electric current** creates a magnetic field around a wire.
    
- **Electric and magnetic fields interact** with moving charges.
    
- Magnetic fields are used in **motors, generators, speakers, and data storage**.
    
- Main formula:
    

$$  
B=\frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}
