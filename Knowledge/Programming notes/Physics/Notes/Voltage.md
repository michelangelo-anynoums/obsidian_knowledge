# 3.2 Voltage

## **Voltage**

- **Voltage** is the **electrical potential difference** between two points.
    
- It gives **energy** to electric charges so they can move.
    
- The **unit of voltage** is the **volt (V)**.
    

**Formula:**

```text
V = W / Q
```

Where:

- **V** = voltage (**V**)
    
- **W** = electrical energy or work (**J**)
    
- **Q** = electric charge (**C**)
    

**Example:**

- A **9 V battery** gives more energy to each charge than a **1.5 V battery**.
    

---

**Alessandro Volta** was the Italian physicist who invented the first electric battery, known as the **voltaic pile**, in 1800.  By stacking alternating discs of zinc and copper separated by brine-soaked material, he created a device that produced a **steady, continuous flow of electric current**.

---

## **Electrical Potential Difference**

- **Potential difference** is another name for **voltage**.
    
- It is the **difference in electrical energy** between two points.
    
- A **larger potential difference** means charges receive **more energy**.
    

**Example:**

- Charges moving through a light bulb lose energy, creating **light** and **heat**.
    

---

## **"Pressure" Pushing Electrons**

- Voltage is often compared to **water pressure** in a pipe.
    
- **Voltage pushes electrons** through a circuit.
    
- **Higher voltage** gives a **stronger push** to the electrons.
    

**Example:**

- A **12 V battery** pushes electrons more strongly than a **1.5 V battery**.
    

> **Remember:**  
> **Voltage pushes. Current flows.**

---

## **Measured in Volts**

- Voltage is measured in **volts (V)**.
    
- A **voltmeter** measures the voltage between two points.
    
- The symbol for voltage is **V**.
    

**Examples:**

- **AA battery:** **1.5 V**
    
- **Car battery:** **12 V**
    
- **USB charger:** **5 V**

# 3.2 Voltage — Resolved Exercises

## Voltage

### Exercise 1

A battery transfers **30 J** of energy to **5 C** of charge. Calculate the voltage.

**Solution:**

Formula:

**V = W / Q**

**V = 30 / 5**

**V = 6 V**

**Answer:** The voltage is **6 V**.

---

### Exercise 2

A **12 V** battery gives energy to **2 C** of charge. How much energy is transferred?

**Solution:**

Formula:

**W = V × Q**

**W = 12 × 2**

**W = 24 J**

**Answer:** The battery transfers **24 J** of energy.
