# 6.1 Wave Basics

## **What is a Wave?**

A **wave** is a **disturbance** that transfers **energy** from one place to another **without moving matter permanently**.

**Examples:**

- Water waves.
- Sound waves.
- Light waves.

---

# **Oscillations in Time and Space**

A **wave** is made of many **oscillations**.

- **Oscillation in time:** A point moves **up and down** as time passes.
- **Oscillation in space:** Different points are at different positions along the wave.

**Example:**  
Imagine shaking one end of a rope.

- The rope moves **up and down** (**time**).
- The wave also travels along the rope (**space**).

# **Amplitude**

The **amplitude** (**A**) is the **maximum distance** from the **equilibrium (rest) position**.

- Larger amplitude → **more energy**.
- Smaller amplitude → **less energy**.

**Unit:** meter (**m**)

**Example:**  
If the highest point is **0.4 m** above the rest position:

A=0.4 m

![[Pasted image 20260723122224.png|522]]

---

# **Frequency**

The **frequency** (**f**) is the **number of oscillations every second**.

**Unit:** **Hertz (Hz)**

### Formula

$$f = 1 / T$$

where:

- **f** = frequency (Hz)
- **T** = period (s)

**Example:**  
A wave makes **5 oscillations in 1 second**.

f=5 Hz

![[Pasted image 20260723122438.png|420]]

---

# **Wavelength**

The **wavelength** (**λ**) is the **distance between two identical points** on a wave.

Examples:

- Crest to crest.
- Trough to trough.

**Unit:** meter (**m**)

![[Pasted image 20260723122513.png|475]]

---

# **Wave Speed**

The **wave speed** is:

$$v=fλ$$

where:

- **v** = wave speed (m/s)
- **f** = frequency (Hz)
- **λ** = wavelength (m)

![[Pasted image 20260723122705.png]]

---

# **Important Ideas**

- **Wave** → transfers **energy**.
- **Amplitude** → maximum displacement.
- **Frequency** → oscillations per second.
- **Wavelength** → length of one complete wave.
- **Wave speed** depends on **frequency** and **wavelength**.

---

# **Resolved Examples**

## **Example 1**

A wave has a **period of 0.5 s**.

**Find the frequency.**

**Solution**

Formula:

$$f=1 / T$$

Substitute:

f=10.5=2 Hz

**Answer:** **2 Hz**

---

## **Example 2**

A wave has:

- **f = 4 Hz**
- **λ = 2 m**

Find the **wave speed**.

**Solution**

Formula:

$$v=fλ$$

Substitute:

v=4×2=8 m/s

**Answer:** **8 m/s**

---

## **Example 3**

The highest point of a wave is **0.25 m** above the rest position.

Find the **amplitude**.

**Solution**

The amplitude is simply the maximum displacement.

A=0.25 m

**Answer:** **0.25 m**
