## Formula

**Ohm's Law**

`V = I × R`

Where:

- **V** = **Voltage** (Volts, **V**)
    
- **I** = **Current** (Amperes, **A**)
    
- **R** = **Resistance** (Ohms, **Ω**)
    

### Rearranged formulas

`I = V / R`

`R = V / I`

---

### Direct proportionality

`I ∝ V`

(Current is directly proportional to voltage when resistance is constant.)

---

## Resolved Example 1

**Find the current.**

Given:

- `V = 12 V`
    
- `R = 4 Ω`
    

Formula:

`I = V / R`

Calculation:

`I = 12 / 4`

`I = 3 A`

**Answer:** **I = 3 A**

---

## Resolved Example 2

**Find the resistance.**

Given:

- `V = 18 V`
    
- `I = 3 A`
    

Formula:

`R = V / I`

Calculation:

`R = 18 / 3`

`R = 6 Ω`

**Answer:** **R = 6 Ω**

---

## Resolved Example 3

**Find the voltage.**

Given:

- `I = 2 A`
    
- `R = 5 Ω`
    

Formula:

`V = I × R`

Calculation:

`V = 2 × 5`

`V = 10 V`

**Answer:** **V = 10 V**
