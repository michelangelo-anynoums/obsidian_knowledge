# 5.3 Electromagnetic Induction

## 1. **Electromagnetic Induction**

![[Pasted image 20260722074716.png|444]]

**Electromagnetic induction** is the process where a **changing magnetic field** creates an **electric current**.

- A **moving magnet** near a wire can produce electricity.
    
- A **changing magnetic field** is needed to induce current.
    

> **No changing magnetic field = no induced current.**

---

## 2. **Changing Magnetic Field Creates Current**

When the magnetic field through a coil **changes**, an **induced current** flows.

This happens when:

- A **magnet moves** into or out of a coil.
    
- The **coil moves** near a magnet.
    
- The **magnetic field changes** in strength.
    

### Formula (Faraday's Law)

$$  
\varepsilon = -\frac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}  
$$

Where:

- **ε** = induced voltage (emf) (**V**)
    
- **Φ₍B₎** = magnetic flux (**Wb**)
    
- **t** = time (**s**)
    

---

## 3. **Transformers and Power Systems**

A **transformer** uses electromagnetic induction to **change voltage**.

- **Step-up transformer** → increases voltage.
    
- **Step-down transformer** → decreases voltage.
    

Power stations use transformers to send electricity **efficiently** over long distances.

### Formula

$$  
\frac{V_s}{V_p}=\frac{N_s}{N_p}  
$$

Where:

- **Vₛ** = secondary voltage
    
- **Vₚ** = primary voltage
    
- **Nₛ** = secondary turns
    
- **Nₚ** = primary turns
    

---

## 4. **Wireless Energy Transfer**

Electricity can be transferred **without wires** using electromagnetic induction.

Examples:

- **Wireless phone chargers**
    
- **Electric toothbrush chargers**
    
- **Some electric vehicle chargers**
    

A changing magnetic field transfers energy from one coil to another.

---

# Resolved Examples

## Example 1

A magnet is moved into a coil of wire.

**Question:** Is a current produced?

### Solution

Yes.

The **changing magnetic field** inside the coil induces an electric current.

**Answer:**

✅ **Yes, an induced current is produced.**

---

## Example 2

A transformer has:

- Primary voltage = **120 V**
    
- Primary turns = **100**
    
- Secondary turns = **200**
    

**Find the secondary voltage.**

### Solution

Formula:

$$  
\frac{V_s}{V_p}=\frac{N_s}{N_p}  
$$

Substitute:

$$  
\frac{V_s}{120}=\frac{200}{100}=2  
$$

$$  
V_s=120\times2=240\ \text{V}  
$$

**Answer:**

$$  
\boxed{V_s=240\ \text{V}}  
$$

---

## Example 3

A phone is placed on a **wireless charger**.

**Question:** How does it receive energy?

### Solution

The charger creates a **changing magnetic field**.

This field induces an **electric current** inside the phone's charging coil.

**Answer:**

✅ **Energy is transferred by electromagnetic induction.**

---

# Quick Summary

- **Electromagnetic induction** happens when a **changing magnetic field creates current**.
    
- A **moving magnet** or **moving coil** can induce electricity.
    
- **Transformers** use induction to increase or decrease voltage.
    
- **Wireless chargers** transfer energy using changing magnetic fields.
    

**Main equations:**

$$  
\varepsilon = -\frac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}  
$$

$$
