# RF Engineering for Security Professionals

## Site Surveys

A **site survey** checks the wireless environment before installing or changing Wi-Fi.  
It identifies **signal strength, interference, channels, and coverage problems**.

## Heatmaps

A **heatmap** is a visual map showing Wi-Fi information across an area.  
It can show **signal strength and coverage**, making weak areas easy to find.

## Channel Planning

**Channel planning** means choosing Wi-Fi channels to reduce **interference** between access points.  
Nearby APs should use channels carefully so they do not **interfere with each other**.

## Capacity Planning

**Capacity planning** focuses on how many **users and devices** the network must support.  
Good capacity planning prevents slow Wi-Fi when many devices are connected.

## High-Density Deployments

A **high-density deployment** is a Wi-Fi environment with **many users and devices in a small area**, such as a stadium, classroom, or conference hall.  
It requires careful **AP placement, channel planning, and capacity planning**.

> **Key idea:** RF security is not only about having a strong signal. It is also about **reducing interference and providing enough capacity** for all devices.
