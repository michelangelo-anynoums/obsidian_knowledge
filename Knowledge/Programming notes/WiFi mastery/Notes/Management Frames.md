# Management Frames

## Beacon Frames

**Beacon frames** are messages sent by an **Access Point**. They announce that the **Wi-Fi network** is available and share information like the **SSID**.

## Probe Requests

A **probe request** is a message sent by a **wireless client** to search for nearby **Wi-Fi networks**.

## Probe Responses

A **probe response** is the reply from an **Access Point**. It gives information about the **Wi-Fi network** so the client can decide whether to connect.

## How Devices Discover Networks

A **wireless client** discovers networks by **listening for beacon frames** or by **sending probe requests**. **Access Points** answer with **probe responses**, allowing the client to find and connect to a network.
