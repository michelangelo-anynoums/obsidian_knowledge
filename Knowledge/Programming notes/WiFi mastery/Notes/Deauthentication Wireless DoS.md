# Deauthentication + Wireless DoS

### Deauthentication Attacks

A **deauthentication attack** sends fake messages that force a device to **disconnect from Wi-Fi**. It can interrupt a user's connection.

### Disassociation Attacks

A **disassociation attack** also forces a device to **leave a Wi-Fi network**. It breaks the connection between the device and the access point.

### RF Jamming Concepts

**RF jamming** uses radio signals to **interfere with Wi-Fi communication**. This can make wireless networks slow or completely unavailable.

### Denial of Service

A **Denial of Service (DoS)** attack tries to make a service or network **unavailable to legitimate users**.
