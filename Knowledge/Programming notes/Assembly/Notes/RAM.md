# RAM (Random Access Memory)

**RAM** is the **temporary memory** of a computer. It stores **data and programs** that the CPU is currently using. RAM is **fast**, but it **loses its data when the computer is turned off**.

## DDR Generations

### DDR3

- **DDR3** is an **older type** of RAM.
    
- It is **slower** and uses **more power** than newer versions.
    

### DDR4

- **DDR4** is **faster** and **more efficient** than DDR3.
    
- It is widely used in **modern computers**.
    

### DDR5

- **DDR5** is the **newest** and **fastest** DDR RAM.
    
- It offers **higher speeds**, **more capacity**, and **better performance**.
    

## Form Factors

### DIMM (Desktop)

- **DIMM** is the **standard RAM module** for **desktop computers**.
    

### SO-DIMM (Laptop)

- **SO-DIMM** is a **smaller RAM module** used in **laptops** and **mini PCs**.
    

## Characteristics

### Capacity

- **Capacity** is the **amount of memory** in the RAM.
    
- It is measured in **GB (Gigabytes)**.
    

### Frequency

- **Frequency** is the **speed** of the RAM.
    
- It is measured in **MHz (Megahertz)**.
    
- A **higher frequency** usually means **faster data transfer**.
    

### Latency

- **Latency** is the **delay** before RAM responds to a request.
    
- **Lower latency** usually means **better performance**.
