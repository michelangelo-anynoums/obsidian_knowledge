# Display Types

## LCD (Liquid Crystal Display)

![[Pasted image 20260710113852.png|385]]

![[Pasted image 20260710113929.png|359]]


- **Traditional flat-panel** display technology.
    
- Uses **liquid crystals** to produce images.
    
- Requires a **backlight**.
    

**Example:** A basic office monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **Dell SE2425H**
    
- **Acer EK241Y**
    

---

## LED (Light Emitting Diode)

![[Pasted image 20260710114021.png|459]]

![[Pasted image 20260710114034.png|300]]

![[Pasted image 20260710114054.png|407]]

- An **LCD display with LED backlighting**.
    
- Brighter and more energy efficient than older LCDs.
    
- The **most common** display type today.
    

**Example:** A standard computer monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **Dell S2721HS**
    
- **Samsung Essential S3**
    

---

## IPS (In-Plane Switching)

![[Pasted image 20260710114150.png|414]]

![[Pasted image 20260710114214.png|306]]

- **Excellent color accuracy**.
    
- **Wide viewing angles**.
    
- Great for **photo editing**, **videos**, and **office work**.
    

**Example:** A graphic design monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **Dell UltraSharp U2723QE**
    
- **LG UltraFine 27UP850-W**
    
- **ASUS ProArt PA279CRV**
    

---

## TN (Twisted Nematic)

![[Pasted image 20260710114251.png|397]]

- **Fast response times**.
    
- Lower **image quality** and **viewing angles**.
    
- Good for **competitive gaming**.
    

**Example:** An esports gaming monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **BenQ ZOWIE XL2411K**
    
- **ASUS VG248QG**
    

---

## VA (Vertical Alignment)

![[Pasted image 20260710114331.png|600]]

- **High contrast ratio**.
    
- Better black levels than IPS.
    
- Good balance between **IPS** and **TN**.
    

**Example:** A home entertainment monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **Samsung Odyssey G5**
    
- **AOC CQ27G2S**
    

---

## OLED (Organic Light Emitting Diode)

![[Pasted image 20260710114359.png|548]]

![[Pasted image 20260710114415.png|345]]
- **Self-emitting pixels** (no backlight).
    
- **Deep blacks**.
    
- **Excellent contrast** and vibrant colors.
    
- Great for **gaming**, **movies**, and **content creation**.
    

**Example:** A premium gaming monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **LG UltraGear 27GS95QE-B**
    
- **ASUS ROG Swift OLED PG32UCDM**
    
- **Alienware AW3225QF**
    

---

## Mini-LED

![[Pasted image 20260710114446.png|301]]

- Uses **many small LED backlight zones**.
    
- Better **HDR performance**.
    
- Higher brightness and better contrast than standard LED displays.
    

**Example:** A high-end professional monitor.

**Display example to buy:**

- **Apple Studio Display (Mini-LED alternative: Pro Display XDR uses advanced backlighting)**
    
- **ASUS ROG Swift PG32UQX**
    
- **Cooler Master Tempest GP27U**
