# Thermal Paste

**Reference:**  [[Thermal paste]]

## Purpose

**Thermal paste** is a material placed **between the CPU/GPU and the cooler**. It fills tiny gaps and helps **move heat** away from the processor.

## Heat Transfer

Thermal paste **improves heat transfer** from the processor to the heatsink. Better heat transfer keeps the CPU or GPU **cooler** and helps it work **more efficiently**.

## Lifespan

Thermal paste does not last forever. Over time, it can **dry out** and become **less effective**, causing higher temperatures.

## Replacement Intervals

Replace thermal paste about **every 2–5 years**, or sooner if the CPU/GPU is **overheating** or the cooler has been removed.
