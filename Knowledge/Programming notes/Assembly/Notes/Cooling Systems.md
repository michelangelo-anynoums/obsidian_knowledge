# Theory

## Cooling Systems

* A **cooling system** removes **heat** from a computer or electronic device.

* It helps keep the device **safe**, **stable**, and **working well**.

## Passive Cooling

* **Passive cooling** uses **no fan**.

* It removes heat with **heat sinks** only.

* It is **silent**, but cools less than active cooling.

## Active Cooling

* **Active cooling** uses a **fan** to help remove heat.

* The fan moves **air** over the heat sink.

* It provides **better cooling** for devices that produce more heat.
