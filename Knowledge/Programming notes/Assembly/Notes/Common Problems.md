## Common Problems

### Slow Performance

The computer works **slower than normal**.

**Possible causes:**

- **HDD:** An old HDD is slower than an SSD and can make the system slow.
- **Insufficient RAM:** Not enough RAM makes it difficult to run several programs at once.
- **Overheating:** High temperature can reduce CPU speed to protect the computer.

### Overheating

The computer becomes **too hot**, which can reduce performance or cause shutdowns.

**Possible causes:**

- **Dust:** Dust blocks airflow and prevents proper cooling.
- **Old thermal paste:** Old paste transfers heat less effectively.
- **Fan failure:** A broken or slow fan cannot remove enough heat.

### Random Shutdowns

The computer **turns off unexpectedly** without warning.

**Possible causes:**

- **Thermal protection:** The computer shuts down to prevent damage from high temperatures.
- **Battery issues:** A damaged or weak battery can cause sudden power loss.
- **Power delivery issues:** Problems with the charger, power supply, or motherboard can interrupt power.
