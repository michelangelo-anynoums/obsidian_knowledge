### USCF Class Ratings (Below 2000)

The United States Chess Federation uses alphabetical classes for players below Expert level:

- **Class J**: 100–199 (Novice)
    
- **Class I**: 200–449
    
- **Class H**: 400–599
    
- **Class G**: 600–799
    
- **Class F**: 800–999
    
- **Class E**: 1000–1199
    
- **Class D**: 1200–1399
    
- **Class C**: 1400–1599
    
- **Class B**: 1600–1799
    
- **Class A**: 1800–1999
    

### FIDE and Professional Titles (2000+)

Above 2000, ratings correspond to official titled statuses and skill tiers:

- **2000–2199**: **Expert** (USCF) / Strong Amateur
    
- **2200–2299**: **Candidate Master (CM)** / National Master range
    
- **2300–2399**: **FIDE Master (FM)**
    
- **2400–2499**: **International Master (IM)**
    
- **2500+**: **Grandmaster (GM)**
    
- **2700+**: **Super-Grandmaster** / World Elite
    
- **2800+**: Top 50–100 players globally
    
- **3000+**: Theoretical maximum; no player has achieved this (highest recorded was 2882 by Magnus Carlsen in 2014).
