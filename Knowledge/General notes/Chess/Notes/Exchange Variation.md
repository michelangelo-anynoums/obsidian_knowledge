In the **Ruy Lopez Exchange Variation** (4.Bxc6), White damages Black's pawn structure by creating doubled c-pawns.  White's plan is to play **d4**, exchange pieces, and win the resulting pawn ending—a scenario former World Champion **Max Euwe** proved favorable for White. 

Although Black gains the **bishop pair** as compensation, the line is considered solid rather than highly ambitious.  Despite this, World Champions **Emanuel Lasker** and **Bobby Fischer** successfully used it to secure victories.
