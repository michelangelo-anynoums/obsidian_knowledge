# Wireless Monitoring

## Wireless IDS

**Wireless IDS (Intrusion Detection System)** monitors wireless networks for **suspicious or unauthorized activity**.  
It **detects and alerts** the administrator but normally does not stop the attack.

## Wireless IPS

**Wireless IPS (Intrusion Prevention System)** detects suspicious activity and can **automatically take action** to stop or block it.  
It provides more **active protection** than an IDS.

## Logging

**Logging** means recording important network events, such as **login attempts, connected devices, and detected attacks**.  
Logs help administrators **investigate problems and security incidents**.

## Detection Strategies

Detection strategies are methods used to find **wireless threats**. Common methods include:

- **Monitoring unknown devices** connected to the network.
- Looking for **rogue access points**.
- Detecting **unusual traffic or behavior**.
- Checking logs for **repeated failed attempts or suspicious activity**.
