# 9.1 Light as Electromagnetic Wave

## **Light as an Electromagnetic (EM) Wave**

- **Light** is a **high-frequency electromagnetic (EM) wave**.
- It is made of changing **electric fields** and **magnetic fields**.
- These fields move together through space.
- **Light does not need a material medium**, so it can travel through a **vacuum**.

> **Example:** Sunlight travels through the empty space between the Sun and Earth.

---

## **High-Frequency EM Wave**

- **Frequency ($f$)** tells us how many waves pass in **1 second**.
- Light has a **very high frequency**, much higher than radio or microwave waves.
- The SI unit of frequency is **hertz (Hz)**.

### **Equation**

$$
f=cλf=\frac{c}{\lambda}
$$
Where:

- **$f$** = frequency (Hz)
- **$c$** = speed of light = **$3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$**
- **$\lambda$** = wavelength (m)

---

## **Travels in Straight Lines**

- In a **uniform medium**, light travels in **straight lines**.
- This is called **rectilinear propagation of light**.
- Shadows are formed because light moves in straight paths.

> **Example:** A tree blocks sunlight and creates a shadow behind it.

---

## **Important Facts**

- ✅ **Light is an electromagnetic wave.**
- ✅ **Light has a very high frequency.**
- ✅ **Light travels at $3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$ in vacuum.**
- ✅ **Light travels in straight lines in a uniform medium.**

# **Resolved Examples**

## **Example 1: Find the frequency**

A light wave has a wavelength of **$600\ \text{nm}$**.

### Given

- $c=3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$
- $\lambda=600\times10^{-9}\ \text{m}$

### Solution

$$
f=cλ; f=\frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

$$
f=3.0×108600×10−9f=\frac{3.0\times10^8}{600\times10^{-9}}
$$

$$
f=5.0×1014Hzf=5.0\times10^{14}\ {Hz}
$$

**Answer:** **$5.0\times10^{14}\ \text{Hz}$**

---

## **Example 2: Find the wavelength**

A light wave has a frequency of **$6.0\times10^{14}\ \text{Hz}$**.

### Given

- $c=3.0\times10^8\ \text{m/s}$
- $f=6.0\times10^{14}\ \text{Hz}$

### Solution
$$
λ=cf; \lambda=\frac{c}{f}
$$
$$
λ=3.0×1086.0×1014\lambda=\frac{3.0\times10^8}{6.0\times10^{14}}
$$

$$
λ=5.0×10−7m\lambda=5.0\times10^{-7}\ {m}
$$

$$
λ=500nm\lambda=500\ {nm}
$$

**Answer:** **$500\ \text{nm}$**


---

## **Example 3: Straight-line travel**

A wall blocks the light from a lamp.

### Question

Why is there a shadow behind the wall?

### Solution

- Light **travels in straight lines**.
- The wall blocks the light rays.
- No light reaches the area behind the wall.

**Answer:** A **shadow** forms because light travels in **straight lines**.
