# 6.6 Modulation

## **What is Modulation?**

**Modulation** is the process of **adding information to a wave** so it can be transmitted.

A **carrier wave** is changed to carry information such as:

- Sound.
- Data.
- Images.

**Example:**  
A radio station uses a carrier wave to send music through the air.

---

# **Why is Modulation Used?**

Modulation helps signals:

- Travel **long distances**.
- Reduce **interference**.
- Share communication channels.

It is used in:

- **Radio**
- **Wi-Fi**
- **Mobile networks**
- **Bluetooth**

---

# **AM (Amplitude Modulation)**

In **AM**, the **amplitude** of the wave changes.

- The **height** of the wave carries the information.
- The **frequency stays constant**.

**Example:**

AM radio changes the amplitude of radio waves to send audio.

---

# **FM (Frequency Modulation)**

In **FM**, the **frequency** of the wave changes.

- The **number of waves per second changes**.
- The **amplitude stays constant**.

**Example:**

FM radio changes the frequency of radio waves to send music.

FM usually has:

- Better sound quality.
- Less noise.

---

# **Digital Modulation**

**Digital modulation** sends information using **binary data**:

0 and 1

The wave is changed to represent digital information.

Used in:

- **Wi-Fi**
- **4G/5G**
- **Bluetooth**
- **Satellite communication**

---

# **Important Ideas**

- **Modulation** = encoding information into a wave.
- **AM** changes **amplitude**.
- **FM** changes **frequency**.
- **Digital modulation** sends **binary data**.
- Modulation allows modern **wireless communication**.

---

# **Resolved Examples**

## **Example 1**

A radio signal changes the **height of its waves** to send information.

**Which modulation is used?**

**Solution**

Changing the wave height means changing the **amplitude**.

**Answer:** **AM (Amplitude Modulation).**

---

## **Example 2**

A radio station changes the number of wave cycles per second to send information.

**Which modulation is used?**

**Solution**

The number of cycles per second is the **frequency**.

**Answer:** **FM (Frequency Modulation).**

---

## **Example 3**

A Wi-Fi signal sends information as groups of **0s and 1s**.

**Which type of modulation is used?**

**Solution**

Wi-Fi uses digital information.

**Answer:** **Digital modulation.**
