# 11.2 Antennas

## 📡 What is an antenna?

An **antenna** is a device that can **send or receive radio waves**.

It connects **electrical signals** with **electromagnetic waves**.

## 📤 Transmission

During **transmission**, an antenna converts an **electrical signal into radio waves**.

**Example:** A Wi-Fi router sends an electrical signal to its antenna. The antenna produces **radio waves** that carry the data to your device.

## 📥 Reception

During **reception**, an antenna converts **radio waves into an electrical signal**.

**Example:** A phone receives a radio signal through its antenna and converts it into an electrical signal that the phone can process.

## 🔑 Important idea

An antenna can work as a **transmitter**, a **receiver**, or both.

- **Transmitter:** electrical signal → radio wave
- **Receiver:** radio wave → electrical signal
