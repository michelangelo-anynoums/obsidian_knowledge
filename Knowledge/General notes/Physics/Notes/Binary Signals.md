## What are Binary Signals?

A **binary signal** is a signal that has only **two possible states**.

- **0 = Low voltage**
    
- **1 = High voltage**
    

Computers use these two states to **store, process, and send data**.

> **Binary** means **only two values**.

---

## Physical Representation of Data

Inside a computer, data is represented by **electrical voltages**.

- **Low voltage → 0**
    
- **High voltage → 1**
    

These voltages travel through electronic circuits and allow the computer to understand information.

---

## Why is Binary Used?

Binary is used because electronic devices can **easily detect two voltage levels**.

**Advantages:**

- **Simple**
    
- **Reliable**
    
- **Fast**
    
- **Less affected by electrical noise**
    

---

## Easy Example

Imagine a **light switch**:

- **OFF** → **0** (low voltage)
    
- **ON** → **1** (high voltage)
    

A computer works in a similar way, but with electrical signals instead of a switch.

---

# Physics Equations

### Voltage

$$  
V = \frac{W}{Q}  
$$

Where:

- **(V)** = voltage (V)
    
- **(W)** = energy (J)
    
- **(Q)** = charge (C)
    

---

### Ohm's Law

$$  
V = I \times R  
$$

Where:

- **(V)** = voltage (V)
    
- **(I)** = current (A)
    
- **(R)** = resistance (Ω)
    

Voltage creates the **high (1)** and **low (0)** electrical signals used by computers.

---

# Resolved Example 1

### Question

A wire has **5 V**.

Is it more likely to represent **0** or **1**?

### Solution

- **0 = low voltage**
    
- **1 = high voltage**
    

Since **5 V** is a **high voltage**, it represents:

**Answer:** **1**

---

# Resolved Example 2

### Question

A digital circuit changes from **5 V** to **0 V**.

What binary values are sent?

### Solution

- **5 V → 1**
    
- **0 V → 0**
    

**Answer:** **1 → 0**

---

# Resolved Example 3

### Question

A circuit has:

- Low voltage = **0 V**
    
- High voltage = **3.3 V**
    

What binary sequence is represented by:

**3.3 V, 0 V, 3.3 V**

### Solution

- **3.3 V → 1**
    
- **0 V → 0**
    
- **3.3 V → 1**
    

**Answer:** **101**

---

# Key Points

- **Binary signals** have **two states**.
    
- **0 = low voltage**.
    
- **1 = high voltage**.
    
- Data is stored as **electrical voltages**.
    
- Computers use binary because it is **simple, reliable, and fast**.
