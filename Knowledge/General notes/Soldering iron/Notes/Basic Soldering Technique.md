# 4. Basic Soldering Technique

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ng_PM9tNJRg&t=222s
### Step-by-step process

1. **Heat the joint**, not the solder first.
2. Apply **solder to the heated joint**.
3. Let the solder **flow naturally**.
4. Remove the **solder first**, then the iron.
5. Let the joint **cool without movement**.

### Good solder joint characteristics

- **Shiny** surface (for leaded solder).
- **Smooth and cone-shaped**.
- Fully covers the **pad and wire**.
- Makes a **strong electrical connection**.

### Bad solder joints

- **Cold joint** → dull or grainy; weak connection.
- **Excess solder** → too much solder forms a **blob**.
- **Insufficient solder** → too little solder; **weak connection**.
